## Recommendation
- Keep `tc_raw_plus` as the baseline public candidate: it is simple, readable, and already has strong texture.
- Test `tc_ubb_plus` as a detail toggle/readout, not the headline; it barely changes the board except where intentional walks matter.
- Avoid heavy BB reweighting as the headline unless you want a deliberately harsher command-purity stat; it can push good-but-wild arms down.
- The two-axis version is product-friendly for archetypes, but the quotient is more distinctive as a compact headline.
- Use predictive RMSE only as appendix: command-only models remain the accuracy benchmark, but TC+ is better as a descriptive identity stat.
