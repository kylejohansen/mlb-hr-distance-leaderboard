## Initial Read
- Men-on K-BB is the cleanest skill read for traffic response: it measures strikeout/walk command specifically after traffic exists.
- RISP K-BB is more on-brand for stranding, but smaller samples make it noisier; use it as context until multi-year stability is tested.
- Strand/LOB should remain a context outcome, not an input, unless paired with traffic-response skill to identify supported vs unsupported strand rates.
- Candidate product split: Traffic Control+ = prevention/expected traffic; Traffic Response+ or Jam Command+ = command after traffic.
